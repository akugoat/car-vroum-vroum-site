Privacy Policy

What the app does

Car vroum vroum runs on its owner’s computer. It uses TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API (and the Instagram API) to upload the owner’s videos to the owner’s own accounts.

Data the app accesses

  • Basic account information returned at sign-in (account ID, display name, avatar), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens, used only to upload videos and check their publishing status.
  • Posting settings returned by TikTok (allowed privacy levels, maximum video length), used to prepare each upload.

Where the data is stored

Tokens are stored only in a local file on the owner’s computer, readable only by the owner’s user account. The app has no server, no database and no analytics.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. Data is only exchanged with TikTok and Instagram (Meta) through their official APIs.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner deletes the local file or revokes access. You can revoke the app’s access at any time in your TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) or Instagram settings (Apps and websites); the stored tokens then stop working.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
